# Plan 12-01: Backend Comprehensive Production Data Seeder

## Goal
Implement an idempotent, realistic `DataSeeder.java` `CommandLineRunner` in Spring Boot that pre-populates the MySQL/H2 database on startup with complete, authentic campus data: buildings, floors, rooms, faculty, timetables, mess menus, hackathons, maintenance notices, community issues, and default test credentials.

## Key Seed Entities
1. **Accounts**:
   - Admin: `admin@uniyaar.edu` / `Admin@123` (`ROLE_ADMIN`)
   - Staff: `facilities@uniyaar.edu` / `Staff@123` (`ROLE_STAFF`)
   - Student: `student@uniyaar.edu` / `Student@123` (`ROLE_STUDENT`)
   - Faculty: `ramesh.sharma@uniyaar.edu`, `ananya.gupta@uniyaar.edu`, etc. (`ROLE_FACULTY`)
2. **Spatial Hierarchy**:
   - University: Apex Institute of Technology
   - 4 Buildings with real campus coordinates, 5 Floors per building, 20+ categorized Rooms (Labs, Lecture Halls, Auditoriums, Faculty Cabins).
3. **Faculty & Timetables**:
   - 8 faculty members with cabins and weekday scheduled timetable slots.
4. **Food & Dining**:
   - Central Student Mess (Annapurna), Campus Cafe & Nescafe, Night Canteen with Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner items and VEG/JAIN/NON_VEG tags.
5. **Events & Hackathons**:
   - UniHacks 2026, AI Agents Workshop, Aarohan Cultural Fest, Cricket Finals, FOSS Meet.
6. **Maintenance & Issue Desk**:
   - Elevator #2 outage with North Wing elevator suggestion, AC chiller repair, water cooler flush.
   - Community issues with real student upvotes and staff resolution notes.
7. **Notices & Circulars**:
   - Urgent rain advisory, Spring 2026 exam timetable, Google/Microsoft placement drive.
