# Plan 09-01: Backend Announcements & Campus Notification Engine

## Goal
Implement entities, repositories, DTOs, business services, and REST endpoints for official campus announcements, emergency alerts, placement notifications, and hostel notices with priority tiering, pinned order, and target audience tagging.

## Key Changes
1. **Enums**:
   - `AnnouncementPriority`: `URGENT`, `IMPORTANT`, `GENERAL`, `CLUB`.
   - `AnnouncementCategory`: `ACADEMIC`, `PLACEMENT`, `EXAM`, `HOSTEL`, `TRANSPORT`, `ADMINISTRATIVE`, `EMERGENCY`.
   - `AnnouncementAudience`: `ALL`, `STUDENTS`, `FACULTY`, `HOSTELLERS`, `DEPARTMENT_SPECIFIC`.
2. **Entity**:
   - `Announcement`: id, title, content, priority, category, targetAudience, department, author, attachmentUrl, externalLink, isPinned, expiresAt, createdAt.
3. **DTOs & Repository**:
   - `AnnouncementResponse`, `AnnouncementCreateRequest`.
   - `AnnouncementRepository` with queries for pinned first, unexpired notices, category & priority filtering.
4. **Service & Controller**:
   - `AnnouncementService`, `AnnouncementController` (`GET /api/announcements`, `GET /api/announcements/urgent`, `GET /api/announcements/{id}`, `POST /api/announcements`).
5. **SecurityConfig**:
   - Public GET access for `/api/announcements/**`.
